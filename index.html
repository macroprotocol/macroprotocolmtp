Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Macro Protocol credit card work?

+

Macro Protocol credit cards function like traditional credit cards but are backed by cryptocurrency. They allow you to make purchases using your crypto assets as collateral while earning rewards in MACRO tokens.

What are the requirements to qualify?

+

Unlike traditional credit cards, our approval process is decentralized and primarily based on your crypto holdings. You'll need to stake a minimum amount of supported cryptocurrencies to establish your credit line.

Are there any fees associated with the card?

+

Macro Protocol aims to be transparent with fees. There are no annual fees or hidden charges. A small transaction fee applies for crypto-to-fiat conversions at the point of sale.

What cryptocurrencies are supported?

+

We support major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and of course our native MACRO token. Additional cryptocurrencies will be added based on community voting and governance decisions.
